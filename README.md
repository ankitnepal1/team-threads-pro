# Jersey Hub

I want to build a modern e-commerce website.

GOAL

Build a professional online jersey store called "Jersey Hub" that sells football and cricket jerseys. The website should look similar to premium sports stores with a clean layout, large product images, category sections, and easy ordering.

AUDIENCE

Football and cricket fans in Nepal and internationally who want club jerseys, national team jerseys, and custom jerseys. Most users will browse on mobile phones, so the website must be mobile-friendly.

PRODUCTS

• Real Madrid Jerseys

• Barcelona Jerseys

• Manchester United Jerseys

• Liverpool Jersey

• PSG Jerseys

• Nepal National Team Jerseys

• International Club Jerseys

• Football Jerseys

• Cricket Jerseys

• Custom Name & Number Jerseys

FEATURES

1. Professional navigation bar at the top.

2. Large hero banner showcasing football jerseys.

3. Shop By Category section with image cards.

4. Product grid with jersey image, name, price, available sizes, and order button.

5. Search and filter by club, country, sport, and size.

6. Featured Products section.

7. Customer Reviews section.

8. WhatsApp floating order button.

9. Contact section and store location section.

10. Responsive design for mobile, tablet, and desktop.

COMPETITIVE ADVANTAGES

• Custom Name & Number Printing

• Multiple Sizes Available

• High Quality Printing

• Cash On Delivery (COD)

• Attractive Packaging

• Fast Customer Support

• Real Product Photos & Videog

DESIGN REQUIREMENTS

• Modern sports e-commerce design.

• Similar layout to professional jersey stores.

• Green navigation bar.

• White and light grey background.

• Premium football theme.

• Smooth hover animations.

• Large product images.

• Rounded cards with shadows.

• Professional typography.

• Fast loading website.

SECTIONS

1. Header/Navbar

2. Hero Banner

3. Shop By Category

4. Featured Products

5. All Products

6. Why Choose Jersey Hub

7. Customer Reviews

8. Delivery & COD Information

9. Contact Information

10. Footer

CONTACT DETAILS

Keep contact number, email, WhatsApp number, and location as placeholders because they will be added later.

TECHNICAL REQUIREMENTS

• Use React + Tailwind CSS.

• Fully responsive

• SEO-friendly.

• Optimized for mobile devices.

• Clean and professional code structure.

OUTPUT

Generate a complete, production-ready e-commerce website for Jersey Hub with modern UI, category cards, product cards, responsive design, and WhatsApp ordering functionality.
